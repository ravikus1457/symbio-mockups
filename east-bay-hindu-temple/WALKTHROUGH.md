## The one-line pitch
> "Your content is great — the site just isn't showing it. Everything the temple actually publishes lives
> inside three images: the Shravan flyer, the 2026 festival calendar, and the sponsorship tiers. We pulled

## Why this matters (talking points)
- **Most devotees visit on a phone.** The current site is hard to read on mobile. This is mobile-first.
- **Your current flyer is the first thing people see** — a slideshow at the top of the home page, with the
  Shravan Shiv Pooja dates, what's included, and both sponsorship tiers.
- **All 116 dates from your 2026 festival poster are now real, searchable text** — filterable by month,
  with the muhurat windows. "Next at the temple" updates itself; it can never go stale the way
  *"Current Flyer 2024"* has.
- **The pages that are empty today now do something:** Book Online takes a real request, Please Join Us
  has a newsletter and a volunteer list, Puja Services lists what Pandit Ji actually performs.
- **It still feels like a temple.** Traditional maroon, gold and saffron; Om accents; classical serif headings.

## What we need from Pandit Ji
1. **Confirm the 2026 calendar dates.** Ours is transcribed from the printed poster and the print is soft in
   places. Two entries look like poster errors worth checking: **Hanuman Jayanti appears twice** (4/01 and
   11/07) and **Naag Panchami appears twice** (8/16 and 12/13). Weekday labels on the site are calculated
   from the dates, so a few differ from the poster's printed weekdays.
2. **Confirm the "also commonly requested" ceremonies** — Satyanarayan Katha, Griha Pravesh, Abhishekam,
   Namkaran & Mundan, Graha Shanti, Antim Sanskar — and whether house visits are offered.
3. **Dakshina / pricing**, if he wants any of it published.
4. **The Cash App handle**, if Cash App should stay on the donate page (the current site names it but
   publishes no handle or QR).
5. **Is `info@eastbayhindutemple.com` monitored?** On the live site it is a hidden mailto target while the
   visible text says punditrakesh@yahoo.com — we've used the yahoo address throughout until he confirms.
6. **Real murti photographs**, to replace the public-domain Raja Ravi Varma paintings in the gallery.
