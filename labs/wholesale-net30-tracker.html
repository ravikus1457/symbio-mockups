Stop chasing wholesale buyers for overdue invoices

Shows exactly what each wholesale account owes and how late it is, across all their Shopify B2B orders. Friendly-to-firm reminder emails go out on a schedule you set, and the tool stops automatically once Shopify marks the invoice paid.
