About Us

Shuei-do Manju

Nestled in San Jose, Shuei-do Manju has been a cherished local gem for decades, upholding the art of Japanese confectionery. We meticulously handcraft each delicate manju and chewy mochi daily, using only the finest ingredients and family recipes passed down through generations, ensuring every bite is a taste of heritage and unparalleled freshness. We're proud to continue this sweet tradition for our community.
