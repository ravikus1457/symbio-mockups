Get ten real testers on your MVP

A guided 10-day sprint that turns your MVP into a testable offer and puts it in front of people who match your user profile. You get the outreach copy, the target list, the interview script, and a scoreboard of who replied, booked, and actually used the product.

  • Generates a one-page test offer plus 3 cold DM/email variants from your product description
  • Builds a vetted list of 100 matching people and communities (subreddits, Slack/Discord, LinkedIn roles) with posting rules per venue
  • Runs a reply tracker: sent → replied → booked → onboarded → used twice, so you see drop-off instead of guessing
  • Auto-drafts a 12-question problem interview script and summarizes recorded calls into a go/pivot/kill memo
No spam. One email when it's ready, one chance to shape it.
