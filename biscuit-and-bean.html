About Us

Biscuit and Bean

Nestled in the heart of Los Angeles, Biscuit and Bean is more than just a cafe – it's a neighborhood haven where quality ingredients meet heartfelt hospitality. We believe in the simple pleasure of a perfectly brewed cup of coffee alongside a warm, scratch-made biscuit, creating moments of comfort and connection for our community. Come gather, refuel, and experience the warmth we pour into every single item.
