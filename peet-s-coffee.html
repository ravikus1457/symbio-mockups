About Us

Peet's Coffee

Peet's Coffee has been a San Francisco institution for nearly 60 years, built on a commitment to sourcing exceptional single-origin beans and mastering the art of the roast. Our baristas are passionate craftspeople who take pride in every espresso shot, pour-over, and cappuccino that leaves our counter. Whether you're a regular fueling your commute or a coffee explorer discovering your new favorite origin, we've got a seat for you.
