About Us

Matterhorn

Matterhorn brings the warmth of the Swiss Alps to San Francisco with authentic fondue, raclette, and traditional mountain fare. Our cozy wood-beamed dining room and welcoming atmosphere make every meal feel like a gathering with friends. Whether you're a local regular or visiting for the first time, you'll find honest food, generous pours, and genuine hospitality.

🕒 Dinner daily 5pm–11pm; Closed Mondays
