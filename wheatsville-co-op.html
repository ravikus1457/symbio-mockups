About Us

Wheatsville Co-op

Wheatsville Co-op is Austin's longest-running member-owned grocery store, dedicated to providing the freshest organic produce, sustainable products, and locally-made goods. We're more than a supermarket—we're a gathering place for neighbors who care about what they eat and where it comes from. By shopping with us, you're supporting local farmers, small producers, and the Austin community.
